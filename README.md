Overview: Surge Stitch is a groundbreaking automated suturing system designed to revolutionize wound closure in medical procedures. By addressing a critical issue in surgical care—medical errors associated with suturing—Surge Stitch significantly enhances patient safety and outcomes. This project is not just an innovation; it’s a potential lifesaver that could reshape the landscape of robotic surgery.

Tackling Medical Errors and Preventing Deaths
Medical errors, particularly those involving wound management, are a silent yet deadly epidemic. It is estimated that 4.2 million deaths occur globally each year due to surgical complications, many of which are directly tied to improper wound closure. Infection and wound dehiscence—where sutures fail and wounds reopen—are two of the leading causes of these complications. Surge Stitch aims to mitigate these risks by automating the suturing process, thereby reducing the incidence of errors and improving patient outcomes.

Alarming Statistics:

4.2 million deaths annually linked to suturing wound complications. Sepsis, often a consequence of wound infection, affects over 1 million people in the U.S. alone each year.
Surgical wound complications like reopening or infection happen in up to 10-20% of major surgeries, with 30-50% of those leading to severe infection and life-threatening sepsis.
Approximately 5 billion people lack access to safe surgical care, contributing to millions of preventable deaths each year.
Wound reopening increases the risk of sepsis by fourfold, and patients who suffer from sepsis have a 50% chance of dying. By ensuring precise and effective closure of wounds, Surge Stitch could significantly lower these numbers, making a profound impact on global health.

Methodology Behind Surge Stitch
Wound Detection and Stitch Point Calculation: Utilizing sophisticated Python algorithms, our system analyzes wound boundaries with over 95% accuracy. This ensures that stitches are placed optimally based on wound characteristics, promoting better healing.

Robotic Design and Arduino Coding: The robotic system employs a unique “printing” method for suturing, allowing for simplified operation and precise control. When scaled down, the Surge Stitch system could fit into a compact device, resembling a band-aid, ensuring portability and ease of use.

Innovative Non-Invasive Stitching Technique: Our unique approach to wound closure utilizes a quick-cooling, strong adhesive material to bond wound edges together. This non-invasive technique minimizes discomfort and scarring while achieving secure attachment, addressing a significant concern in traditional suturing methods. Why did we do this? Well, robots unfortunately cannot perform such precise needle and string actions without becoming BIG, so we were forced to create our own portable method that was just as efficient.
